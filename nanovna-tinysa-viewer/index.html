Frequently asked questions

How do I open a NanoVNA .s1p or .s2p file in Mergen Scope?

Open the app and drag your NanoVNA .s1p or .s2p file into the workspace. Mergen Scope plots S11/S21 magnitude, phase, VSWR, return loss and group delay, and displays reflections on an interactive Z/Y Smith chart.

Does Mergen Scope handle tinySA CSV exports cleanly?

Yes. Packed tinySA CSV traces are parsed directly and gaps in the sweep are preserved rather than connected with misleading straight lines. Mixed-separator and custom column files open through the import wizard.

Can I compare NanoVNA antenna tuning sweeps side by side?

Yes. Drop multiple .s1p or .s2p files into the same workspace to overlay S11 return loss or VSWR curves, view both traces on the Smith chart, or compute differences with trace math.

Does Mergen Scope connect directly to my NanoVNA or tinySA over USB?

No. Mergen Scope is an offline file viewer and analysis workspace—capture files on the instrument SD card or with NanoVNA-Saver / tinySA-App, then drop them into Mergen Scope for analysis and clean figure export.
