Mergen Scope

Open Any Waveform. Analyze Everything.

Mergen Scope is a browser-based waveform and measurement analysis workspace. Open DAT, CSV, TXT, and Touchstone files today, then inspect traces with markers, reference lines, trace math, RF tools, saved sessions, and multi-pane comparison.

Features

Open Most Waveforms*
Load DAT, CSV, TXT, and Touchstone measurement data today, with broader waveform support as the goal.
Plot Traces
Inspect waveform, spectrum, and S-parameter traces with responsive plotting controls.
Analyze Signals
Use markers, reference lines, trace math, RF helpers, and derived traces.
Compare Panes
Compare traces in stacked panes with shared X navigation and independent Y scaling.
Export Results
Export visible traces as CSV and capture chart views as PNG or SVG.
Save Workspaces
Save and reload complete sessions with files, panes, markers, lines, and analysis state.

*Mergen Scope is being built toward broader waveform format support. Current supported formats are DAT, CSV, TXT, and Touchstone.

Supported Formats

Format Notes
DAT Rohde & Schwarz spectrum analyzer exports and supported tabular DAT files
CSV Wizard-assisted comma-delimited time or frequency data
TXT Wizard-assisted text tables with comma, semicolon, or tab delimiters
Touchstone .s1p-.sNp network data with S/Y/Z traces, Smith chart view, and RF analysis

Why I Built This

I'm an electrical engineer, not a web developer. I wanted a practical way to inspect spectrum analyzer exports, Touchstone files, and simple instrument tables directly in the browser. The first version was a small viewer; the current app is closer to a lightweight RF and signal analysis workspace.

Rohde & Schwarz .dat exports are still the best-tested path, and support has expanded to generic tabular files and Touchstone network data. If you find bugs, have suggestions, or want to contribute, feel free to open an issue.
