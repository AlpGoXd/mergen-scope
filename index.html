Your files stay on your computer

Mergen Scope runs entirely in your browser. When you open a file, your browser reads it; nothing is uploaded, because there is no server to upload to. There are no cookies, trackers or third-party fonts. The app remembers your settings (theme, size, panel layout) in your browser’s local storage, and they never leave your computer. The only other requests it makes are for the demo files, from this site.

The app, working

Markers. Peak and next-peak search, delta markers measured from any marker, and reference lines that show where they cross each trace.

Panes. Up to four, side by side: a chart, a table or a Smith chart. Panes with the same X unit zoom together; a time capture zooms on its own.

Analysis. Bandwidth, ripple, channel power, IP3 / TOI, noise density, FFT, stability factors, peak tables, smoothing and trace maths.

Workspaces. Save everything (files, panes, markers, analyses) to one file and open it again later, or send it to a colleague.

Formats

ExtensionWhat it isWhat you get
.s1p … .sNpTouchstone files from network analysers and simulators, with any number of ports.S, Y, Z and ABCD; dB, magnitude, phase, real and imaginary; VSWR, return loss and group delay; a Smith chart for reflections.
.datInstrument exports: spectrum analyser sweeps, scope records and other column data, with or without a header.The trace on its real axis. Header fields (RBW, reference level, detector, start and stop) go into the file’s Info table and rebuild the axis when a column is missing.
.csv · .txt · .tsvScope captures, logs and anything else in columns, separated by commas, semicolons or tabs.Time or frequency on X with real units, and a column wizard when the layout is not obvious.
WorkspaceA file saved from Mergen Scope.Your files, panes, markers, lines and analyses, as you left them.
The contents decide how a file is read, not the extension.

Export

Export redraws the chart at the size you choose instead of taking a screenshot, as PNG up to 600 dpi or as SVG that stays vector. Keep the pane exactly as it looks in the app, use a Paper preset (88.9 mm for one IEEE column, 181.9 mm for two, 8 pt serif text, a hollow symbol on each trace for black-and-white print), or copy the figure as LaTeX and let pgfplots draw it in your document.
