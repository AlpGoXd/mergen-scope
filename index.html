Frequently asked questions

How do I open an oscilloscope CSV file in my browser?

Open the app and drop the CSV file into the workspace. The import wizard lets you map columns and units if needed.

Can I view a Touchstone .s2p file and its Smith chart online?

Yes. Open the app and drop in the .s2p file to view its S-parameters and Smith chart.

Can I open Rohde & Schwarz FSV or FSVA .dat files in my browser?

Yes. Drop an ASCII Rohde & Schwarz spectrum analyzer .dat export into Mergen Scope to inspect traces along with span, RBW and detector metadata locally in your browser, with no upload.

Is Mergen Scope tied to one instrument brand?

No. Any VNA that exports Touchstone (.s1p through .s16p) files and any spectrum analyzer or oscilloscope that exports CSV, TSV, TXT or ASCII .dat tables can be opened, with the import wizard handling delimiters, columns and units.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Files are read and processed in your browser and are not uploaded. Settings such as the theme stay in your browser.

Which instruments and file formats work?

Mergen Scope accepts spectrum analyzer files (including Rohde & Schwarz .dat and tabular CSV/TXT sweeps), VNA Touchstone .s1p through .s16p files, and oscilloscope CSV, TSV and TXT column data. R&S .dat has been tested with FSVA3044 exports; generic tables may need column and unit mapping.

Does Mergen Scope require installation or an account?

No installation or account is required. Open the app in your browser to use it.

Can I use Mergen Scope offline in an air-gapped lab?

Yes. The production build is a single self-contained app.html file with no CDN requests or server backend, so a local copy opened from disk works completely offline.

Is Mergen Scope free?

Yes. Mergen Scope is free and open source under the Apache-2.0 license.

Can I export plots for a paper or LaTeX?

Plots can be exported as PNG, SVG, PDF or LaTeX (TikZ); CSV data export is also available.

Can I compare traces or do trace math?

Yes. You can compare traces and use trace math to add, subtract, multiply or divide two traces.

It is a beta. Tell me what breaks.

A file that does not open, a measurement you miss, a number that looks wrong: all of it helps.
