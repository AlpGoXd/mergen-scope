Frequently asked questions

Which oscilloscope brands work with Mergen Scope?

Any oscilloscope that exports text tables (CSV, TSV or TXT)—including Rigol, Siglent, Keysight, Tektronix and Rohde & Schwarz—works through the built-in import wizard, which detects delimiters, header rows, X/Y columns, units and multipliers with a live plot preview. Proprietary binary waveform files (.wfm, .isf) must be exported as CSV first.

Can I compute FFT, THD, SNR and ENOB from an oscilloscope CSV capture?

Yes. Run Resample if your timestamps are uneven, then add the FFT card (14 window functions, averaging, max hold, detrend and dBV/dBm/density scales). Adding Harmonics to that FFT trace reports THD, SFDR, THD+N, SNR, SINAD and ENOB using the exact window noise bandwidth.

What happens to blank or non-numeric metadata rows in oscilloscope CSVs?

Header rows are skipped automatically in the import wizard, and blank or non-numeric cells are skipped cleanly rather than turned into false zeros.

Are large oscilloscope CSV files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser with zero uploads. Files over 50 MB ask for confirmation before opening, and files over 500 MB are refused to protect browser memory.
