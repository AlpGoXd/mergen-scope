## Evidence and limits

The comparison places both retained Phase 1 app screenshots and both Phase 2 app screenshots next to **10-tokens** and **03-armed-messages-analysis-cards**. Phase 2 light/dark controls are also compared with both boards. Retained screenshots are 1440 × 900; the reference boards are 1440 × 1900 and 1440 × 2870. Images are linked at native resolution. Different demo data, board annotations, and board composition are not product defects. These are existing evidence captures, not newly captured app states. A screenshot without an armed tool or open Analysis panel cannot prove those states conform.

Root reviewed images and source; Terra independently audited token adoption and recaptured the dark toast; Luna independently measured PNG colours. Source references below are at the checkpoint, so line numbers may move later. RGB/hex values here describe measured pixels, not new component colour literals. Text antialiasing, gradients, opacity and compositing prevent equating every pixel with a CSS token. Uniform surface samples and declarations are the useful checks.

Reference README and matching boards must be embedded in task briefs; legacy white mixes and absent states remain open in [deferred.md](deferred.md).
