View Touchstone .s1p/.s2p files and the Smith chart online

Open Touchstone files from a VNA, including .s1p through .s16p networks.

Network views

View S, Y and Z parameters, and ABCD for two-port networks. Display formats include dB, magnitude and phase. VSWR, return loss and group delay are also available as views.

Smith chart

The Smith chart has a frequency range bar and readouts including impedance Z, reflection coefficient Γ and VSWR.

Network analysis

For two-port networks, Stability reports K and μ, and Reciprocity / Isolation compares S21 and S12. See the analysis guide for details and limitations.
