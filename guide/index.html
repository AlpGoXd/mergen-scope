Mergen Scope user guide

Open measurement files in a browser workspace, arrange traces in panes and inspect them with markers, lines and analysis cards.

Files and panes

Drop a file into the workspace. CSV, TXT and TSV columns, R&S DAT spectrum exports and Touchstone networks are supported. Arrange traces in up to four panes, or use the experimental eight-pane layout; X axes can be linked.

Traces, markers and lines

Files supply traces that can be displayed in panes. Normal and delta markers, peak and minimum search, and reference lines help inspect traces. Reference lines can list their intersections with traces.

Analysis and export

Analysis cards report measurements or create new traces. Math traces follow their inputs; use ⋯ › Freeze as a copy to keep a fixed copy. Export plots as PNG, SVG, PDF or LaTeX (TikZ), or export CSV data. Save a workspace as JSON to restore its data, panes, markers, lines and analyses.
