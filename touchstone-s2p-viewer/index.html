Frequently asked questions

How many ports do Touchstone files support in Mergen Scope?

Mergen Scope opens Touchstone 1.x and 2.0 files from 1-port (.s1p) through 16-port (.s16p) in RI, MA or dB/angle formats with arbitrary reference impedance Z0.

Does the Smith chart show both impedance (Z) and admittance (Y)?

Yes. You can switch between Z, Y and combined Z+Y grids. In Z+Y mode, hovering highlights constant-resistance/reactance and dashed constant-conductance/susceptance arcs and displays Z, Y, reflection coefficient Gamma (with Z0) and VSWR.

Can I compute Rollett K stability and reciprocity from an .s2p file?

Yes. For any two-port Touchstone file, the Stability card reports minimum K, minimum mu and mu', |Delta| bounds and unstable point counts, while Reciprocity / Isolation reports worst and average |S21 - S12| error and worst-case isolation.

Is my .s2p file safe for confidential or NDA RF designs?

Yes. All parsing and math run locally in your browser with zero uploads and zero trackers. You can also save the single-file app.html build and run it completely offline.
